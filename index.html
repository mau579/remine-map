Before you go

Prepare your batteries for a safer drop-off.

A few simple checks can protect you, location staff, and the materials being collected.

01

Check the battery

Do not transport a battery that is hot, smoking, leaking, swollen, or badly damaged. Follow the location’s instructions instead.

02

Protect the terminals

Keep batteries separated. Tape exposed terminals on lithium-ion, rechargeable, button-cell, and 9V batteries when recommended.

03

Confirm before leaving

Call the listed location to verify accepted types, hours, limits, fees, and any special packaging requirements.

Not sure what kind of battery you have?

Start with the label on the battery or device, then ask the drop-off location before transporting it.

Battery Safety

Battery recycling can involve safety risks. Always contact a listed location before bringing batteries or electronics.

  • Do not bring damaged, leaking, swollen, smoking, or hot batteries unless the location specifically accepts them.
  • Keep batteries away from heat, fire, water, children, and pets.
  • Keep loose batteries separated when possible.
  • Tape battery terminals when recommended, especially rechargeable, lithium-ion, button cell, and 9V batteries.
  • When unsure, call the drop-off location first.
